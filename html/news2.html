Jana Nayagan Release Issue

The highly anticipated Tamil film Jana Nayagan, starring actor-turned-politician Thalapathy Vijay, has been delayed from its original January 9 release due to a major certification dispute with the Central Board of Film Certification (CBFC). After the Madras High Court initially directed the CBFC to grant a U/A 16+ certificate, a Division Bench stayed that order, leading the film’s producers to approach the Supreme Court to challenge the stay and push for timely clearance. The certification row, which involves objections over certain scenes and has now escalated into a legal battle at the highest judicial level, has left the film’s release date uncertain and sparked widespread discussion in the film industry.
